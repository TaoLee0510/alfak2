# Protocol v2: exact continuous division validation

This protocol was fixed before opening any test landscapes. The earlier tau-leap development data and its negative/tradeoff results remain unchanged as a model-robustness experiment.

- Formal calibration and test generation use `prepare_pilot.R --backend gillespie`, with exponential waiting times at the total population birth rate, parent probabilities proportional to population × birth rate, correlated daughter copy-number changes, and removal of nonviable daughters. It has no simulation time-step parameter.
- The frozen biological/scenario parameters are four chromosomes, copy numbers 1–3, beta 0.005, interval 1.5, initial population 1,200 and sample depths 150/150. The full 81-node target domain is identical across methods. Each dataset uses a single t0-count-weighted observed-reference fitness contrast.
- The landscape seed manifest reserves replicates 1–2 for development, 3–4 for calibration and 5–8 for test, separately in each of three landscape families. Each landscape has two ABM and two sampling replicates. Formal calibration therefore contains six independent landscapes and 24 datasets. Test remains unopened until the calibration objects are frozen.
- The candidate is the separately snapshotted `candidate-v1` source/DLL with its MD5 manifest, using the joint count pipeline, division-process forward model and Laplace inference. The fixed prior SDs are baseline 0.5, main 0.07, curvature 0.04, interaction 0.04, residual 0.125 and edge 0.25. No formal calibration/test truth may alter these point-estimation settings.
- Uncalibrated intervals propagate the complete latent covariance through 8,000 independently seeded posterior draws and then the fixed reference contrast. Empirical calibration only changes interval endpoints; reported fitness means and SDs remain unchanged.
- Calibration uses signed standardized errors separately at each reference distance. Landscapes have equal mass, datasets within each landscape have equal mass, and nodes within each dataset/distance have equal mass. The signed empirical quantiles set 50%, 80% and 95% interval endpoints.
- Calibration objects record the exact model/argument fingerprints and scenario fields: depth, beta, interval, chromosome count, copy-number bounds, reference definition and generating protocol. Mismatches are rejected. Unknown distances receive missing calibrated intervals and an explicit unvalidated status.
- Six calibration landscapes support empirical calibration only. No finite-sample conformal coverage guarantee or guarantee across landscape families is claimed. Test evaluation retains distance/family failures and compares proper interval scores, nominal coverage, point errors and complete prediction coverage jointly.
- The separate five-chromosome beta-zero support-limit probe covers d0–d5. Its remote fitness is not data-identified, and the four-chromosome calibrated intervals cannot be applied to it.

The simulator check is reproducible with `validate_simulation_backends.R`. It compares beta-zero exact population means against the Yule formula and verifies the expected small-step convergence of the historical tau scheme.
